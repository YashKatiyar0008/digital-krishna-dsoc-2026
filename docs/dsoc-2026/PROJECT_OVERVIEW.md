# Digital Krishna AI — Project Overview

## DSOC Summer Edition 2026

**Tagline:** Ancient Wisdom for Modern Challenges

**Primary track:** Artificial Intelligence & Intelligent Systems

**Live prototype:** https://www.digitalkrishna.co.in

---

## Project Summary

Digital Krishna AI is a spiritual guidance and reflective learning
platform that helps users understand modern personal challenges
through empathetic conversation, culturally grounded teachings,
practical reflection, stories, and multilingual experiences.

The platform is inspired by the guidance Krishna gave Arjuna during
a period of confusion, emotional conflict, and uncertainty.

Digital Krishna does not claim to speak directly for Krishna. It uses
technology to help users explore reviewed teachings and reflect on
their situations responsibly.

---

## Problem Statement

Students and young adults regularly experience challenges such as:

- Academic pressure
- Career uncertainty
- Fear and anxiety
- Anger and frustration
- Loneliness
- Lack of motivation
- Confusion about important decisions
- Difficulty maintaining discipline
- Emotional exhaustion

Many people want culturally familiar guidance but may find traditional
spiritual texts difficult to search, understand, or apply to modern
situations.

General-purpose AI systems may also provide generic responses,
incorrect quotations, or unsupported scripture references.

---

## Proposed Solution

Digital Krishna combines conversational AI, scripture retrieval,
multilingual interaction, practical reflection, educational stories,
and responsible-AI controls in one web platform.

Users can describe their concerns naturally and receive a structured
response containing:

- Empathetic acknowledgement
- Relevant spiritual or philosophical guidance
- A scripture reference when a reviewed source is available
- A simple explanation of the teaching
- Practical reflection steps
- A personal reflection question
- Responsible-use messaging when appropriate

---

## Target Users

Digital Krishna is designed for:

- Students
- Young adults
- Spiritual learners
- Families
- Educators
- People interested in Indian philosophy
- Users seeking culturally familiar reflection tools

---

## Core Features

### 1. Saathi AI Companion

Users can describe personal concerns in natural language and receive
structured, empathetic, and practical guidance.

### 2. Scripture-Grounded Retrieval

The platform searches a curated scripture collection to identify
teachings relevant to the user’s concern.

### 3. Source Integrity

Reviewed scripture content is separated from AI-generated
interpretation.

Generated wording must not be presented as an authentic scripture
quotation.

### 4. Practical Reflection

Responses may contain:

- Small daily actions
- Reflection questions
- Breathing or grounding suggestions
- Gratitude exercises
- Study or discipline steps
- Relevant teachings

### 5. Multilingual Experience

Digital Krishna supports experiences in:

- English
- Hindi
- Hinglish
- Romanised Hindi

### 6. Scripture Library

Users can explore teachings from sources such as:

- Bhagavad Gita
- Ramcharitmanas
- Selected Upanishadic teachings
- Curated Krishna stories

### 7. Stories and Educational Media

The platform includes:

- Krishna stories
- Illustrations
- Animations
- Hindi and English narration
- Teaching summaries
- Real-life lessons

### 8. Responsive Web Experience

The platform is designed for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
