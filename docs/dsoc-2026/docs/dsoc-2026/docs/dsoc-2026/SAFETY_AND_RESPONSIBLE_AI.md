# Digital Krishna AI — Safety and Responsible AI

## DSOC Summer Edition 2026

Digital Krishna AI is designed as a spiritual reflection, cultural
learning, and educational platform.

It does not replace qualified medical, psychological, legal,
financial, or emergency professionals.

---

## 1. Responsible AI Principles

Digital Krishna prioritises:

- User safety
- Scripture integrity
- Cultural respect
- Transparency
- Privacy
- Human oversight
- Clear limitations
- Responsible personalisation
- Accessibility
- Multilingual inclusion

---

## 2. Platform Role

Digital Krishna may provide:

- Spiritual reflection
- General wellbeing guidance
- Relevant reviewed teachings
- Practical reflection exercises
- Educational stories
- Motivation and encouragement
- Questions that support thoughtful decision-making

Digital Krishna must not present itself as:

- A doctor
- A therapist
- A lawyer
- A financial adviser
- An emergency service
- A religious authority
- A replacement for trusted real-world support

The platform must not claim that generated responses are direct
messages from Krishna.

---

## 3. Scripture Integrity

Scripture accuracy is essential to user trust.

The platform separates:

1. Reviewed scripture text
2. Reviewed translation
3. Reviewed teaching summary
4. AI-generated explanation
5. Practical reflection suggestions

The AI must not invent:

- Verses
- Chapter numbers
- Quotations
- Scripture references
- Translation sources

When a reviewed reference is unavailable, the platform should clearly
state that no verified source was retrieved.

---

## 4. Source Attribution

When scripture content is displayed, available metadata should include:

- Scripture name
- Chapter
- Verse number
- Original text
- Translation language
- Review status

AI-generated explanations should be visually and structurally
separated from authentic source content.

---

## 5. Sensitive-Content Handling

The system may check user input for language involving:

- Immediate danger
- Severe emotional distress
- Abuse or exploitation
- Threats toward another person
- Medical emergencies
- Illegal or dangerous requests

When a serious risk is detected, the normal spiritual-guidance flow
should be limited.

The platform should encourage users to contact an appropriate trusted
person, qualified professional, or local emergency service.

The system must not provide instructions that could increase danger.

---
